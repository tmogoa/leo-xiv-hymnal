Ucwebile

zu: Ucwebile en: Holy Holy Holy (Sanctus)
verse 1
Ucwebile, ucwebile, ucwebile Nkulunkulu Holy, holy, holy Lord God of hosts
Osithandayo You who love us
Wen' okhwez' izinto zonke You who sustain all things
Maw' sibheke ngobubele Look upon us with kindness
verse 2
Usikhweze ngomsa wakho Raise us up by your grace
Thin' esingabantu bakho We who are your people
Mhla simuka kulomhlaba On the day we depart this world
Uzusamukele Baba Receive us Father
verse 3
Sithokoze njalonjalo That we may rejoice forever
Embusweni waphezulu In your kingdom above
Sikutuse sikubonge Praising you and giving thanks
Kanye nengelosi zonke Together with all the angels
