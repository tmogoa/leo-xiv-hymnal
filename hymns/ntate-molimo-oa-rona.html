Ntate Molimo oa Rona

st: Ntate Molimo oa Rona en: Father Our God
verse 1
Ntate, Molimo oa rona Father, our God
Re ananela ho uena We offer this to you
Ka hostia e hloekileng Through this holy host
Ka eona re fe tshoarelo Grant us through it forgiveness
Le grasia tsa poloko And the graces of salvation
Fa ba phelang le ba shoeleng For both the living and the dead
Bophelo ba bo sa feleng Life that has no end
